# Premium Tax Credit (Form 1095-A)

Place your Form 1095-A here if you had Marketplace health insurance.

## What Is Form 1095-A?

The Health Insurance Marketplace sends Form 1095-A if you or a family member enrolled in coverage through Healthcare.gov or a state Marketplace.

## Why It Matters

Form 1095-A is used to:
1. Claim the Premium Tax Credit
2. Reconcile advance payments of the credit
3. Potentially get a refund or owe additional tax

## What to Look For

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| A | Monthly enrollment premiums |
| B | Monthly SLCSP premium |
| C | Monthly advance payment of PTC |

**SLCSP** = Second Lowest Cost Silver Plan (used for calculation)

## Premium Tax Credit

The PTC helps pay for health insurance purchased through the Marketplace.

### How It Works

- You can get the credit in advance (paid directly to insurer)
- Or claim the full credit when you file
- At filing, you reconcile advance payments vs. actual credit

### Income Requirements

Credit is available at all income levels (no upper limit since ARP expansion).

Amount is based on:
- Household income
- Family size
- Cost of benchmark plan (SLCSP)

## Reconciliation

| If Advance Payments Were... | Result |
|-----------------------------|--------|
| Less than actual credit | You get a refund |
| More than actual credit | You may owe repayment |
| Equal to actual credit | No adjustment |

## What to Include

- Form 1095-A from the Marketplace
- Records of any changes in income or family size during year
- Documentation if you claim exemption from coverage

## Form 8962

Use Form 8962 to reconcile the Premium Tax Credit.
