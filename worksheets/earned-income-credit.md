# 2025 Earned Income Credit (EIC) Worksheet

## Overview

The Earned Income Credit is a refundable tax credit for low-to-moderate income workers.

---

## Eligibility Requirements

### Basic Requirements (All Filers)

1. **Earned Income**: Must have earned income from work
2. **AGI Limit**: Below threshold for your number of children
3. **Investment Income**: Cannot exceed $11,600
4. **Filing Status**: Cannot be Married Filing Separately
5. **Citizenship**: U.S. citizen or resident alien all year
6. **SSN**: Must have valid SSN for yourself, spouse, and qualifying children
7. **Not a Dependent**: Cannot be claimed as dependent on another return
8. **Foreign Income**: Cannot file Form 2555 (Foreign Earned Income)

### Without Qualifying Children (Additional Requirements)

- Age 25-64 at end of tax year (or any age if specified student)
- Cannot be a qualifying child of another taxpayer
- Lived in U.S. more than half the year

---

## Qualifying Child Requirements

A child must meet ALL tests:

### 1. Relationship
- Son, daughter, stepchild, adopted child, foster child
- Brother, sister, stepbrother, stepsister
- Descendant of any above (grandchild, niece, nephew)

### 2. Age
- Under 19 at end of year, OR
- Under 24 and full-time student, OR
- Any age if permanently disabled

### 3. Residency
- Lived with you in U.S. for more than half the year

### 4. Joint Return
- Child cannot file joint return (unless only to claim refund)

### Step 2: Calculate Earned Income

```

3. Net self-employment income:                    $_______
4. Other earned income:                           $_______
5. Total earned income (add Lines 2-4):           $_______
```

### Step 3: Check Investment Income

```
6. Taxable interest:                              $_______
7. Tax-exempt interest:                           $_______
8. Ordinary dividends:                            $_______
9. Capital gains (net):                           $_______
10. Royalties/rental income (passive):            $_______
11. Total investment income (add Lines 6-10):     $_______

If Line 11 > $11,600, you cannot claim EIC. STOP.
```

### Step 4: Determine Credit Amount

Use EIC tables in IRS Publication 596 or the simplified calculation below:

```
For 0 children:
  Phase-in: 7.65% of earned income up to $8,260
  Maximum: $632
  Phase-out: Begins at $10,330 (single) / $17,250 (MFJ)
  Phase-out rate: 7.65%

For 1 child:
  Phase-in: 34% of earned income up to $12,390
  Maximum: $4,213
  Phase-out: Begins at $22,720 (single) / $29,640 (MFJ)
  Phase-out rate: 15.98%

For 2 children:
  Phase-in: 40% of earned income up to $17,400
  Maximum: $6,960
  Phase-out: Begins at $22,720 (single) / $29,640 (MFJ)
  Phase-out rate: 21.06%

For 3+ children:
  Phase-in: 45% of earned income up to $17,400
  Maximum: $7,830
  Phase-out: Begins at $22,720 (single) / $29,640 (MFJ)
  Phase-out rate: 21.06%
```

### Step 5: Enter Credit

```
12. EIC amount (from table or calculation):       $_______
    (Enter on Form 1040, Line 27)
```

## Common Disqualifying Situations

1. **Investment income over $11,600**
2. **Filing as Married Filing Separately**
3. **No valid SSN** (ITIN doesn't qualify)
4. **Non-resident alien** part of year
5. **Claiming foreign earned income exclusion**
6. **Someone else claims you as dependent**

---

## Forms Required

- **Schedule EIC**: List qualifying children
- **Form 1040**: Line 27 (Earned Income Credit)

## Important Notes

- EIC is **fully refundable** - you get the credit even if you owe no tax
- If claiming EIC, you cannot also claim the Credit for Other Dependents for the same child
- IRS may delay refunds for returns claiming EIC until mid-February
