## Introduction

Tax credits directly reduce your tax liability. There are two types:

| Type | Effect |
|------|--------|
| **Nonrefundable** | Can reduce tax to zero, but no refund from excess |
| **Refundable** | Can result in a refund even if you owe no tax |

## Earned Income Credit (EIC)

### What Is the EIC?

The EIC is a **refundable credit** for low-to-moderate income workers. It's especially beneficial for families with children.

### 2025 Maximum Credits

| Qualifying Children | Maximum EIC |
|--------------------|-------------|
| 3 or more | $7,830 |
| 2 | $6,960 |
| 1 | $4,213 |
| None | $632 |

### 2025 Income Limits

| Children | Single/HOH | MFJ |
|----------|-----------|-----|
| 3+ | $59,899 | $66,819 |
| 2 | $55,768 | $62,688 |
| 1 | $49,084 | $56,004 |
| 0 | $18,591 | $25,511 |

### Requirements

**You must have:**
- Earned income
- Valid SSN for you, spouse, and qualifying children
- Meet the income limits
- Not be a qualifying child of another person
- U.S. citizenship or resident alien for entire year

**You cannot have:**
- Investment income over $11,600 (2025)
- Foreign earned income exclusion (Form 2555)

### Qualifying Child for EIC

Must meet **all** of these tests:
- **Relationship:** Child, stepchild, foster child, sibling, or descendant
- **Age:** Under 19, or under 24 if full-time student, or any age if permanently disabled
- **Residency:** Lived with you in the U.S. for more than half the year
- **SSN:** Valid SSN

### EIC Without Qualifying Children

You may qualify if you:
- Are at least age 25 but under 65
- Cannot be claimed as a dependent
- Lived in the U.S. more than half the year
- Have earned income and AGI under limits

### Main Home in United States (2025)

**New:** Check the box on Form 1040/1040-SR if your main home was in the United States for over half the year. This helps determine EIC eligibility.

## Child and Dependent Care Credit

### What Is This Credit?

A credit for expenses you pay for the care of a qualifying person so you (and your spouse) can work or look for work.

### Maximum Credit

| AGI | Credit Percentage | Maximum Expenses |
|-----|------------------|------------------|
| Up to $15,000 | 35% | $3,000 (1 person) / $6,000 (2+) |
| Over $43,000 | 20% | $3,000 (1 person) / $6,000 (2+) |

**Maximum credit:** $1,050 (one qualifying person) or $2,100 (two or more)

### Qualifying Person

- Your dependent child under age 13
- Your spouse who is physically or mentally unable to care for themselves
- Any dependent who is physically or mentally unable to care for themselves

### Qualifying Expenses

**Includes:**
- Daycare
- Babysitters
- Nursery school
- Day camp
- Before/after school care

**Does NOT include:**
- Overnight camp
- Food
- Clothing
- Education (kindergarten and above)
- Medical care

### Form 2441

Use **Form 2441** to claim this credit.

## Education Credits

### American Opportunity Credit

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Maximum credit | $2,500 per student |
| Refundable | 40% (up to $1,000) |
| Years available | First 4 years of postsecondary education |
| Student requirement | At least half-time |

**Income phase-out (2025):**
| Filing Status | Phase-Out Range |
|--------------|-----------------|
| Single/HOH | $80,000 - $90,000 |
| MFJ | $160,000 - $180,000 |

**Qualifying expenses:**
- Tuition
- Required fees
- Course materials (books, supplies, equipment)

### Lifetime Learning Credit

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Maximum credit | $2,000 per return |
| Refundable | No |
| Years available | Unlimited |
| Student requirement | None (even one course) |

**Income phase-out (2025):**
| Filing Status | Phase-Out Range |
|--------------|-----------------|
| Single/HOH | $80,000 - $90,000 |
| MFJ | $160,000 - $180,000 |

### Form 8863

Use **Form 8863** to claim education credits.

### Cannot Claim Both

You cannot claim both credits for the **same student** in the same year.

## Retirement Savings Contributions Credit

### Saver's Credit

A credit for contributions to retirement accounts.

### Maximum Credit

| AGI (2025) | Credit Rate | Max Credit |
|-----------|-------------|------------|
| Single: Up to $23,750 | 50% | $1,000 |
| Single: $23,751-$25,625 | 20% | $400 |
| Single: $25,626-$39,500 | 10% | $200 |
| MFJ: Up to $47,500 | 50% | $2,000 |
| MFJ: $47,501-$51,250 | 20% | $800 |
| MFJ: $51,251-$79,000 | 10% | $400 |

### Qualifying Contributions

- Traditional IRA
- Roth IRA
- 401(k), 403(b), governmental 457
- SIMPLE IRA
- SEP IRA

### Requirements

- Age 18 or older
- Not a full-time student
- Not claimed as a dependent on someone else's return

### Form 8880

Use **Form 8880** to claim this credit.

## Adoption Credit

### Maximum Credit (2025)

**$17,280** per eligible child

### Refundable Portion (New for 2025)

Up to **$5,000** of the adoption credit may be refundable. The refundable amount is determined separately for each child.

### Qualifying Expenses

- Adoption fees
- Court costs
- Attorney fees
- Travel expenses
- Other adoption-related expenses

### Special Needs Adoption

For special needs adoptions, you can claim the maximum credit even if actual expenses are less.

**New for 2025:** Tribal government special needs determinations are now recognized equally with state determinations.

### Income Phase-Out (2025)

| MAGI | Effect |
|------|--------|
| Under $259,190 | Full credit |
| $259,190 - $299,190 | Partial credit |
| $299,190 or more | No credit |

### Form 8839

Use **Form 8839** to claim the adoption credit.

## Premium Tax Credit

### What Is the PTC?

A credit to help pay for health insurance purchased through the Health Insurance Marketplace.

### How It Works

- **Advance payments:** Credit paid directly to insurance company monthly
- **Year-end reconciliation:** Compare advance payments to actual credit on tax return

### Eligibility

- Purchased insurance through the Marketplace
- Not eligible for affordable employer coverage or government programs
- Not claimed as a dependent
- If married, must file jointly
- Income requirements met

### Income Requirements

The PTC is available at all income levels (ARP expansion continued).

### Form 8962

Use **Form 8962** to reconcile the premium tax credit.

## Other Credits

### Foreign Tax Credit

For foreign income taxes paid. Generally better than deducting.
- **Form 1116** (if over $300/$600)
- Can claim without form if under limits

### Residential Clean Energy Credit

30% of costs for:
- Solar panels
- Solar water heaters
- Wind turbines
- Geothermal heat pumps
- Battery storage

**Form 5695**

### Energy Efficient Home Improvement Credit

Up to 30% of costs for qualified improvements:
- Insulation
- Windows and doors
- Heat pumps
- Water heaters

**Limits:** Various annual limits apply
**Form 5695**

### Clean Vehicle Credit

Up to $7,500 for qualifying new electric vehicles
Up to $4,000 for qualifying used electric vehicles

**Income limits apply**
**Form 8936**

### Elderly or Disabled Credit

For low-income taxpayers who are:
- Age 65+, or
- Permanently and totally disabled

**Schedule R**
