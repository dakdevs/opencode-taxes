# Charitable Contributions

Place donation receipts and acknowledgment letters here.

## What Qualifies

Donations to qualified 501(c)(3) organizations:
- Religious organizations
- Nonprofit charities
- Educational institutions
- Hospitals
- Government entities

## What Doesn't Qualify

- Political contributions
- Donations to individuals
- Donations to foreign organizations (usually)
- Value of your time/services
- Raffle tickets (except excess over FMV of prize)

## Documentation Requirements

| Donation Amount | Required Documentation |
|-----------------|----------------------|
| Under $250 | Bank record, receipt, or written record |
| $250 or more (cash) | Written acknowledgment from charity |
| Over $500 (noncash) | Form 8283, Section A |
| Over $5,000 (noncash) | Form 8283, Section B + appraisal |

## Cash vs. Noncash Donations

**Cash donations:** Generally deductible up to 60% of AGI

**Noncash donations:** 
- Deduct fair market value (FMV)
- Clothing/household items must be in "good or better" condition
- Keep records of original cost and condition

## What to Include

- Acknowledgment letters from charities
- Bank statements showing donations
- Credit card statements
- Written records with date, amount, organization name
- Receipts for noncash donations with description and FMV
