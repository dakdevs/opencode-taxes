# 1099-G (Government Payments)

Place your 1099-G forms here.

## What Is a 1099-G?

Government agencies send 1099-G for certain payments made to you.

## Common Types

| Box | Description | Tax Treatment |
|-----|-------------|---------------|
| 1 | Unemployment compensation | Fully taxable |
| 2 | State or local tax refund | May be taxable (see below) |
| 3 | Tax year of refund | Year the refund relates to |
| 4 | Federal tax withheld | Credit on return |

## State Tax Refund Rules

Your state tax refund is taxable ONLY if:
1. You **itemized** deductions last year, AND
2. You received a **tax benefit** from the state tax deduction

If you took the standard deduction last year, your state refund is NOT taxable.

## Unemployment Compensation

All unemployment benefits are fully taxable as ordinary income.

You can elect to have tax withheld from unemployment by filing Form W-4V.

## Missing Your 1099-G?

Check your state's tax authority website—many provide 1099-G forms online.
