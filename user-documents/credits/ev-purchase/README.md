# Clean Vehicle Credit

Place electric/clean vehicle purchase documentation here.

## New Clean Vehicle Credit

### Maximum Credit
- **$7,500** for qualifying new electric vehicles

### Requirements

**Vehicle must:**
- Be a new vehicle (not used)
- Have final assembly in North America
- Meet battery component and critical mineral requirements
- Have MSRP under limits ($55,000 cars; $80,000 vans/SUVs/trucks)
- Be placed in service in 2025

**You must:**
- Buy for your own use (not resale)
- Use primarily in the U.S.
- Meet income limits

### Income Limits (Modified AGI)

| Filing Status | MAGI Limit |
|--------------|------------|
| MFJ | $300,000 |
| HOH | $225,000 |
| Single/other | $150,000 |

Can use current or prior year MAGI (whichever qualifies).

## Used Clean Vehicle Credit

### Maximum Credit
- **$4,000** or 30% of sale price (whichever is less)

### Requirements
- Model year at least 2 years old
- Purchase price $25,000 or less
- First transfer to qualified buyer
- Lower income limits apply

## Point-of-Sale Transfer

Starting in 2024, you can transfer the credit to the dealer and get it as a reduction in price at purchase time.

## What to Include

- Purchase agreement/bill of sale
- Window sticker (Monroney label)
- Form 15400 (Clean Vehicle Seller Report)
- VIN number
- Final assembly certification
- Proof of battery requirements met
