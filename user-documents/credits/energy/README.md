# Energy Credits

Place receipts and documentation for energy improvements here.

## Two Types of Energy Credits

### 1. Residential Clean Energy Credit (Form 5695, Part I)

For installing renewable energy systems:

| System | Credit Rate | Limit |
|--------|-------------|-------|
| Solar electric (PV) | 30% | No limit |
| Solar water heating | 30% | No limit |
| Wind turbines | 30% | No limit |
| Geothermal heat pumps | 30% | No limit |
| Fuel cells | 30% | $500/0.5 kW |
| Battery storage (3+ kWh) | 30% | No limit |

### 2. Energy Efficient Home Improvement Credit (Form 5695, Part II)

For energy-efficient improvements:

| Improvement | Credit Rate | Annual Limit |
|-------------|-------------|--------------|
| Insulation, windows, doors | 30% | $1,200 total |
| Heat pumps (air/water) | 30% | $2,000 |
| Biomass stoves | 30% | $2,000 |
| Electric panel upgrade | 30% | $600 |
| Home energy audit | 30% | $150 |

**Annual total limit:** $3,200 (combines all improvements)

## Requirements

### Clean Energy Credit
- Must be for your home in the U.S.
- New property (not used)
- You must own the home

### Home Improvement Credit
- Must be for your main home
- Home must be in the U.S.
- You must own the home
- Must meet specific energy efficiency requirements

## What to Include

- Invoices/receipts for equipment and installation
- Manufacturer's certification statement (for efficiency standards)
- Proof of payment
- For solar: system specifications and interconnection agreement
