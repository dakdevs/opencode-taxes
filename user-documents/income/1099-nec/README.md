# 1099-NEC (Nonemployee Compensation)

Place your 1099-NEC forms here.

## What Is a 1099-NEC?

Clients/businesses send you a 1099-NEC if they paid you **$600 or more** as an independent contractor, freelancer, or self-employed person.

## What to Look For

| Box | Description | Tax Treatment |
|-----|-------------|---------------|
| 1 | Nonemployee compensation | Self-employment income |
| 4 | Federal income tax withheld | Credit on your return |

## Important: Self-Employment Tax

Income on 1099-NEC is subject to **self-employment tax** (15.3%) in addition to income tax.

You'll need to:
1. Report on Schedule C (Profit or Loss from Business)
2. Pay self-employment tax via Schedule SE
3. Potentially make quarterly estimated tax payments

## Business Expenses

If you have 1099-NEC income, you can deduct ordinary and necessary business expenses:
- Home office
- Equipment
- Supplies
- Mileage
- Professional services

Place expense documentation in `deductions/business-expenses/`.

## No 1099-NEC?

You must still report self-employment income even if:
- Payment was under $600
- You didn't receive a 1099-NEC

Keep your own records of all payments received.
