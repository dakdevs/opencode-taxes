# Education Credits

Place education expense documentation here (in addition to Form 1098-T in deductions/tuition).

## Two Education Credits

### American Opportunity Credit (AOC)

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Maximum | $2,500 per eligible student |
| Refundable | 40% (up to $1,000) |
| Years | First 4 years of higher education |
| Enrollment | At least half-time |

### Lifetime Learning Credit (LLC)

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Maximum | $2,000 per return (not per student) |
| Refundable | No |
| Years | Unlimited |
| Enrollment | Even one course |

## You Can't Claim Both

For the same student in the same year, choose AOC or LLC—not both.

## Qualified Expenses

- Tuition
- Required fees
- Books, supplies, equipment (for AOC)

## NOT Qualified Expenses

- Room and board
- Insurance
- Medical expenses
- Transportation
- Personal expenses

## Income Limits (2025)

| Credit | Full Credit | Phase-Out | No Credit |
|--------|-------------|-----------|-----------|
| AOC | Under $80K | $80K-$90K | Over $90K |
| LLC | Under $80K | $80K-$90K | Over $90K |

*Double amounts for MFJ*

## What to Include

- Form 1098-T (also in deductions/tuition)
- Receipts for books and supplies
- Receipts for required equipment
- Student account statements
