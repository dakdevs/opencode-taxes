# Step 8: File Return

**Time estimate:** 15 minutes

## Overview

Now it's time to file your return with the IRS (and state if applicable).

## Filing Options

### E-File (Recommended)

**Advantages:**
- Faster processing (21 days vs. 6-8 weeks)
- Immediate confirmation
- Fewer errors
- Faster refunds with direct deposit

**Options:**
1. **IRS Free File** - Free if AGI ≤ $84,000
2. **Tax Software** - TurboTax, H&R Block, etc.
3. **Tax Professional** - CPA or Enrolled Agent

### Paper Filing

**Mail to:** See [Where to File](../irs-pub17/appendix/where-to-file.md)

**Considerations:**
- Takes 6-8 weeks to process
- Higher error rate
- Use certified mail for proof of filing

## Forms to File

### Everyone Files

- **Form 1040** (or 1040-SR for seniors)

| Situation | Form |
|-----------|------|

| Dependents | Form 2441 |

| Energy credits | Form 5695 |

## Payment Options (If You Owe)

### Electronic Payment

- **IRS Direct Pay** - Free, from bank account
- **EFTPS** - Schedule payments in advance
- **Credit/Debit Card** - Fees apply

### By Check

- Make payable to "United States Treasury"
- Include Form 1040-V (payment voucher)
- Write SSN, phone, "2025 Form 1040" on check

### Can't Pay in Full?

- **Installment Agreement** - Monthly payments
- **Short-term Plan** - Pay within 180 days
- **Offer in Compromise** - Settle for less

## Refund Options

### Direct Deposit (Fastest)

- Refund in ~21 days
- Up to 3 accounts (Form 8888)
- Can deposit to IRA

### Paper Check

- Refund in 6-8 weeks
- Mailed to address on return

### Savings Bonds

- Buy I Bonds with refund
- Use Form 8888

## Important Notes

### Keep Your Records

Keep copies of:
- Filed return (all pages)
- All W-2s and 1099s
- Supporting documentation

**How long:** At least 3 years (7 years if claim loss)

### Confirmation

**E-file:** You'll receive acknowledgment within 24-48 hours

**Paper:** Consider certified mail with return receipt

### Track Your Refund

- **Online:** [IRS.gov/Refunds](https://www.irs.gov/refunds)
- **App:** IRS2Go
- **Phone:** 1-800-829-1954

Wait 24 hours after e-filing (4 weeks after paper filing) to check.

## State Return

If you owe state taxes, file separately with your state tax authority.

Most states have:
- Online filing systems
- Similar deadlines to federal

- Monitor for refund (if applicable)

### E-File Options

| Option | Cost | Who It's For |
|--------|------|--------------|
| IRS Free File | Free | AGI ≤ $84,000 |
| Free File Fillable | Free | Any income (basic forms) |
| Tax Software | $0-$200+ | Most taxpayers |
| Tax Pro | $150-$500+ | Complex situations |

### Payment Methods (If Owed)

| Method | Fee | Time |
|--------|-----|------|
| IRS Direct Pay | Free | 1-2 days |
| EFTPS | Free | Schedule ahead |
| Debit Card | $2-$4 | Immediate |
| Credit Card | ~2% | Immediate |
| Check | Free | Mail time |

**Can't pay?** File anyway! Failure-to-file penalty (5%/month) is 10x worse than failure-to-pay (0.5%/month).

### Refund Timeline

| Filing Method | Refund Method | Typical Time |
|---------------|---------------|--------------|
| E-file | Direct deposit | 21 days |
| E-file | Paper check | 4-6 weeks |
| Paper | Direct deposit | 6-8 weeks |
| Paper | Paper check | 8-10 weeks |

### Extension Filing

**Form 4868** gives 6-month extension to FILE (not to PAY)
- Due: April 15, 2026
- Extended deadline: October 15, 2026
- Still owe penalties/interest on unpaid tax
