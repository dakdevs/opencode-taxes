# HSA Documents (Form 5498-SA)

Place your HSA documents here.

## What Is an HSA?

A Health Savings Account (HSA) is a tax-advantaged account for medical expenses when you have a High Deductible Health Plan (HDHP).

## Triple Tax Advantage

1. **Contributions** are tax-deductible (or pre-tax if through employer)
2. **Growth** is tax-free
3. **Withdrawals** for qualified medical expenses are tax-free

## 2025 Contribution Limits

| Coverage Type | Under 55 | 55 or Older |
|--------------|----------|-------------|
| Self-only | $4,300 | $5,300 (+$1,000) |
| Family | $8,550 | $9,550 (+$1,000) |

## Forms You'll Receive

### Form 5498-SA (Contributions)
- Sent by **May 31**
- Shows contributions made
- Box 2: Total contributions for 2025

### Form 1099-SA (Distributions)
- Shows distributions from HSA
- Box 1: Gross distribution
- Box 2: Earnings on excess contributions
- Box 3: Distribution code

## What to Look For on 5498-SA

| Box | Description |
|-----|-------------|
| 1 | Employer contributions (including payroll) |
| 2 | Total contributions for year |
| 3 | Total HSA contributions this year |
| 4 | Rollover contributions |
| 5 | Fair market value |

## Contribution Sources

- **Employer contributions** (including your payroll deductions) - Box 1
- **Direct contributions** (you made outside of payroll) - Include in total

## What to Include

- Form 5498-SA
- Form 1099-SA (if you took distributions)
- Receipts for medical expenses paid with HSA
- Record of direct contributions made
