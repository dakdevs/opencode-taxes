## Introduction

After you've determined your taxable income (gross income minus adjustments, minus deductions), you need to figure your tax.

**Taxable Income = Gross Income - Adjustments - (Standard or Itemized Deductions)**

## Tax Computation Methods

### Which Method to Use

| Under $100,000 | Tax Tables |
| $100,000 or more | Tax Rate Schedules |

## Tax Tables

### How to Use Tax Tables

1. Find your taxable income
2. Find the row that includes your taxable income
3. Find the column for your filing status
4. The intersection is your tax

## Capital Gains Tax

### Long-Term Capital Gains Rates

Long-term capital gains (assets held more than 1 year) are taxed at preferential rates:

| 2025 Bracket | Tax Rate |
|--------------|----------|
| 10% or 12% ordinary rate | 0% |
| 22%, 24%, 32%, 35% ordinary rate | 15% |
| 37% ordinary rate | 20% |

### 2025 Long-Term Capital Gains Thresholds

| Filing Status | 0% Rate | 15% Rate | 20% Rate |
|--------------|---------|----------|----------|
| Single | $0 - $48,350 | $48,351 - $533,400 | Over $533,400 |
| MFJ/QSS | $0 - $96,700 | $96,701 - $600,050 | Over $600,050 |
| MFS | $0 - $48,350 | $48,351 - $300,025 | Over $300,025 |
| HOH | $0 - $64,750 | $64,751 - $566,700 | Over $566,700 |

### Short-Term Capital Gains

Short-term capital gains (assets held 1 year or less) are taxed as **ordinary income**.

### Special Capital Gains Rates

| Type | Tax Rate |
|------|----------|
| Collectibles (art, coins, etc.) | 28% maximum |
| Unrecaptured Section 1250 gain | 25% maximum |
| Qualified small business stock (Section 1202) | Various exclusions apply |

### Qualified Dividends

Qualified dividends receive the same preferential rates as long-term capital gains.

## Alternative Minimum Tax (AMT)

### What Is AMT?

The AMT is a parallel tax system designed to ensure that taxpayers with substantial income pay at least a minimum amount of tax.

### 2025 AMT Exemption Amounts

| Filing Status | Exemption |
|--------------|-----------|
| Single | $88,100 |
| MFJ/QSS | $137,000 |
| MFS | $68,500 |

### AMT Exemption Phase-Out

The exemption phases out when AMTI (Alternative Minimum Taxable Income) exceeds:

| Filing Status | Phase-Out Begins | Phase-Out Complete |
|--------------|------------------|-------------------|
| Single | $626,350 | $978,750 |
| MFJ/QSS | $1,252,700 | $1,800,700 |
| MFS | $626,350 | $900,350 |

### AMT Tax Rates

| AMTI | Rate |
|------|------|
| First $232,600 ($116,300 MFS) | 26% |
| Above $232,600 ($116,300 MFS) | 28% |

### Common AMT Adjustments

Items that may trigger AMT:
- State and local tax deduction
- Miscellaneous itemized deductions (none allowed for AMT)
- Incentive stock option exercise
- Private activity bond interest
- Certain accelerated depreciation

### Form 6251

Use Form 6251 to calculate your AMT.

## Net Investment Income Tax

### 3.8% Additional Tax

An additional 3.8% tax applies to the lesser of:
- Net investment income, **or**
- Modified AGI exceeding threshold

### MAGI Thresholds

| Filing Status | Threshold |
|--------------|-----------|
| Single | $200,000 |
| MFJ/QSS | $250,000 |
| MFS | $125,000 |
| HOH | $200,000 |

### What Is Net Investment Income?

**Includes:**
- Interest
- Dividends
- Capital gains
- Rental and royalty income
- Non-qualified annuities
- Income from passive activities
- Income from trading financial instruments

**Excludes:**
- Wages and self-employment income
- Social Security benefits
- Tax-exempt interest
- Distributions from qualified retirement plans

### Form 8960

Use Form 8960 to calculate the Net Investment Income Tax.

### Example

```
Filing status: Single
Modified AGI: $250,000
Net investment income: $40,000
Threshold: $200,000

Excess MAGI: $250,000 - $200,000 = $50,000
Lesser of NII ($40,000) or Excess ($50,000) = $40,000
NIIT: $40,000 × 3.8% = $1,520
```

## Self-Employment Tax

### What Is Self-Employment Tax?

Self-employment tax consists of:
- Social Security tax: 12.4% (on first $176,100 of net self-employment income for 2025)
- Medicare tax: 2.9% (on all net self-employment income)

### Additional Medicare Tax

An additional 0.9% Medicare tax applies to self-employment income exceeding:

| Filing Status | Threshold |
|--------------|-----------|
| Single | $200,000 |
| MFJ | $250,000 |
| MFS | $125,000 |

### Calculation

1. Net self-employment income × 92.35%
2. Apply tax rates to the result
3. You can deduct half of SE tax as an adjustment to income

### Form Schedule SE

Use Schedule SE to calculate self-employment tax.

                                       ─────────
