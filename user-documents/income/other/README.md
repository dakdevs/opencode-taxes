# Other Income Documentation

Place other income documents here that don't fit in the specific categories.

## Examples of Other Income

- **Gambling winnings** (W-2G or your records)
- **Jury duty pay**
- **Prizes and awards**
- **Hobby income**
- **Alimony received** (for pre-2019 divorces only)
- **Bartering income**
- **Canceled debt** (1099-C)
- **Foreign income**
- **Tips not reported on W-2**
- **Cash payments received**

## Remember

All income is taxable unless specifically excluded by law. This includes:
- Cash payments
- Barter transactions
- Foreign income
- Income from illegal activities

## What to Include

- Form W-2G (gambling winnings)
- Form 1099-C (canceled debt)
- Records of cash income
- Foreign income statements
- Any other income documentation
