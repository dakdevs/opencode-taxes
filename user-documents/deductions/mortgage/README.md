# Mortgage Interest (Form 1098)

Place your Form 1098 and mortgage statements here.

## What Is Form 1098?

Your mortgage lender sends Form 1098 showing mortgage interest paid during the year.

## What to Look For

| Box | Description | Deductible? |
|-----|-------------|-------------|
| 1 | Mortgage interest received | Yes |
| 2 | Outstanding mortgage principal | For limit calculation |
| 3 | Mortgage origination date | Determines limit |
| 4 | Refund of overpaid interest | Reduces deduction |
| 5 | Mortgage insurance premiums | Maybe (income limits) |
| 6 | Points paid on purchase | Yes (see rules) |

## Deduction Limits

| Mortgage Date | Debt Limit |
|--------------|------------|
| After Dec 15, 2017 | $750,000 |
| Before Dec 16, 2017 | $1,000,000 |

Only interest on debt up to this limit is deductible.

## What Qualifies

Interest on debt used to:
- Buy your main or second home
- Build your home
- Substantially improve your home

## What Doesn't Qualify

Home equity debt used for other purposes (paying off credit cards, vacations, etc.) is NOT deductible.

## Multiple Mortgages?

If you have more than one mortgage (main home + second home), place all 1098s here.
