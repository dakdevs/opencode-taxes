# Tuition Statements (Form 1098-T)

Place your Form 1098-T here.

## What Is Form 1098-T?

Educational institutions send Form 1098-T showing tuition payments and scholarships.

## Used For Education Credits

Form 1098-T is used to claim:
- **American Opportunity Credit** (up to $2,500, 40% refundable)
- **Lifetime Learning Credit** (up to $2,000, nonrefundable)

## What to Look For

| Box | Description | Notes |
|-----|-------------|-------|
| 1 | Payments received | Tuition paid during year |
| 2 | (Reserved) | No longer used |
| 4 | Adjustments for prior year | May affect credit |
| 5 | Scholarships or grants | Reduces qualified expenses |
| 6 | Adjustments to scholarships | Prior year adjustments |
| 7 | Checkbox: amounts for prior year | Timing of payments |
| 8 | At least half-time student | Required for AOC |
| 9 | Graduate student | LLC only for graduate |

## Calculating Qualified Expenses

```
Qualified Expenses = Box 1 - Box 5 + Books/Supplies
```

Books, supplies, and required equipment can be added even if not on 1098-T.

## American Opportunity Credit Requirements

- First 4 years of post-secondary education
- At least half-time enrollment
- Pursuing a degree or credential
- No felony drug conviction

## Lifetime Learning Credit

- Any year of post-secondary education
- Even one course qualifies
- No degree requirement
- Includes courses to improve job skills

## Income Limits (2025)

| Credit | Full Credit | Phase-Out |
|--------|-------------|-----------|
| AOC | Under $80K ($160K MFJ) | $80K-$90K ($160K-$180K) |
| LLC | Under $80K ($160K MFJ) | $80K-$90K ($160K-$180K) |
