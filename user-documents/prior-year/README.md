# Prior Year Documents

Place documents from previous tax years here.

## Why Prior Year Documents Matter

Several tax calculations depend on prior year information:
- Estimated tax safe harbor (based on last year's tax)
- Capital loss carryforwards
- Net operating loss carryforwards
- IRA basis tracking
- Installment sale income

## Folder Contents

### 2024-return/
Your complete 2024 tax return:
- Form 1040 (all pages)
- All schedules filed
- Any attached forms

**Key information from 2024 return:**
- Line 11: Adjusted Gross Income (AGI)
- Line 24: Total Tax
- Schedule D: Capital loss carryover
- Form 8606: IRA basis

### carryovers/
Documents for amounts carried forward:
- Capital loss carryover worksheet
- Net operating loss statements
- Passive activity loss carryovers
- Investment interest expense carryover
- Charitable contribution carryover

## Capital Loss Carryover

If your 2024 capital losses exceeded gains by more than $3,000, you have a carryover.

| 2024 Situation | 2025 Effect |
|----------------|-------------|
| Net loss > $3,000 | Excess carries to 2025 |
| Short-term loss | Carries as short-term |
| Long-term loss | Carries as long-term |

## Prior Year AGI

Your 2024 AGI is needed to:
- E-file (as identity verification)
- Calculate estimated tax safe harbor
- Determine certain phase-outs

If you don't have your 2024 return, you can request a transcript from IRS.
