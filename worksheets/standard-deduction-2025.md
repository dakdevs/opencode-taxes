# 2025 Standard Deduction Amounts

## Base Standard Deduction

| Filing Status | Amount |
|---------------|--------|
| Single | $15,000 |
| Married Filing Jointly | $30,000 |
| Married Filing Separately | $15,000 |
| Head of Household | $22,500 |
| Qualifying Surviving Spouse | $30,000 |

## Additional Standard Deduction (Age 65+ or Blind)

| Filing Status | Per Qualifying Condition |
|---------------|--------------------------|
| Single | $1,950 |
| Head of Household | $1,950 |
| Married Filing Jointly | $1,550 |
| Married Filing Separately | $1,550 |
| Qualifying Surviving Spouse | $1,550 |

**Note**: Each person can qualify for up to 2 additional amounts (one for age 65+, one for blindness).

### Example Calculations

**Single, Age 67**:
- Base: $15,000
- Additional (age): $1,950
- Total: $16,950

**Married Filing Jointly, both spouses 65+**:
- Base: $30,000
- Additional (spouse 1 age): $1,550
- Additional (spouse 2 age): $1,550
- Total: $33,100

**Single, Age 70, Blind**:
- Base: $15,000
- Additional (age): $1,950
- Additional (blind): $1,950
- Total: $18,900

## When to Itemize vs Standard Deduction

**Itemize if your total itemized deductions exceed the standard deduction.**

Common itemized deductions:
- State and local taxes (SALT) - capped at $10,000
- Mortgage interest (on up to $750k of debt)
- Charitable contributions
- Medical expenses exceeding 7.5% of AGI

### Decision Worksheet

```
1. Standard Deduction for your filing status:     $_______
   (Include additional amounts if applicable)

2. Calculate Itemized Deductions:
   a. State/local taxes (max $10,000):            $_______
   b. Mortgage interest:                          $_______
   c. Charitable contributions:                   $_______
   d. Medical (amount over 7.5% of AGI):          $_______
   e. Total Itemized (add a-d):                   $_______

3. Compare:
   - If Line 2e > Line 1: ITEMIZE
   - If Line 1 >= Line 2e: STANDARD DEDUCTION
```

## Special Rules

### Cannot Use Standard Deduction If:

1. Married filing separately and spouse itemizes
2. Filing for short tax year due to accounting period change
3. Nonresident alien or dual-status alien during the year

### Dependent Standard Deduction

If someone can claim you as a dependent, your standard deduction is the **greater of**:
- $1,350, OR
- Earned income + $450 (but not more than the regular standard deduction)

### Disaster Loss Deduction

Taxpayers in federally declared disaster areas may be able to claim net disaster losses as an additional standard deduction. Check IRS announcements for qualifying disasters.
