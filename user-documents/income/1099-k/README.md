# 1099-K (Payment Card and Third Party Network Transactions)

Place your 1099-K forms here.

## What Is a 1099-K?

Payment processors send 1099-K when you receive payments through:
- Credit/debit card transactions
- Payment apps (PayPal, Venmo, Cash App, Zelle)
- Online marketplaces (eBay, Etsy, Amazon)

## 2025 Reporting Threshold

For 2025, you'll receive a 1099-K if:
- Gross payments exceed **$20,000**, AND
- Number of transactions exceeds **200**

## Important Notes

### Not All 1099-K Is Taxable Income

The 1099-K reports **gross payments received**, which may include:
- Business income (taxable)
- Personal reimbursements from friends (NOT taxable)
- Items sold at a loss (NOT taxable as income)

### Avoid Double-Counting

If you also received 1099-NEC for the same income, don't report it twice.

## What to Look For

| Box | Description | Notes |
|-----|-------------|-------|
| 1a | Gross amount | Total payments processed |
| 1b | Card not present transactions | Online payments |
| 2 | Merchant category code | Type of business |
| 4 | Federal tax withheld | Backup withholding (if any) |

## Personal Item Sales

If you sold personal items (garage sale items, used goods):
- Only report GAIN (sale price minus what you paid)
- Personal items sold at a loss = no income to report
- Keep records of original cost
