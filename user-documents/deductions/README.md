# Deductions Documentation

Place documents for tax deductions in the appropriate subfolders.

## Standard vs. Itemized Deductions

You'll either take the **standard deduction** OR **itemize** your deductions—whichever is greater.

### 2025 Standard Deduction

| Filing Status | Amount |
|--------------|--------|
| Single | $15,750 |
| Married Filing Jointly | $31,500 |
| Married Filing Separately | $15,750 |
| Head of Household | $23,625 |

### When to Itemize

Itemize if your total itemized deductions exceed your standard deduction. Common scenarios:
- Large mortgage interest
- High state/local taxes (up to $40,000 limit)
- Significant charitable donations
- Major medical expenses

## Deduction Categories

| Folder | Deduction Type | Schedule A Line |
|--------|---------------|-----------------|
| `mortgage/` | Mortgage interest | Line 8a |
| `property-tax/` | Real estate taxes | Line 5b |
| `state-local-tax/` | State/local income or sales tax | Line 5a |
| `charitable/` | Charitable contributions | Lines 11-14 |
| `medical/` | Medical expenses (over 7.5% AGI) | Line 1 |
| `student-loan/` | Student loan interest | Schedule 1 (adjustment) |
| `tuition/` | Tuition (for credits) | Form 8863 |
| `business-expenses/` | Self-employment expenses | Schedule C |

## New 2025 Deductions (Schedule 1-A)

These are available even if you take the standard deduction:
- No tax on tips (up to $25,000)
- No tax on overtime (up to $12,500)
- No tax on car loan interest (up to $10,000)
- Enhanced senior deduction (up to $6,000)
