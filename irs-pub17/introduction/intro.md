## About This Publication

Publication 17 provides a comprehensive guide to your federal income tax. It covers the general rules for filing a federal income tax return and explains the tax rules that apply to individuals.

---

## How This Publication Is Arranged

This publication is organized into four main parts:

### Part One - The Income Tax Return
Covers filing requirements, filing status, dependents, and withholding.

### Part Two - Income and Adjustments to Income
Covers various types of income and adjustments you can make to reduce your taxable income.

### Part Three - Standard Deduction, Itemized Deductions, and Other Deductions
Covers deductions you can take to reduce your taxable income.

### Part Four - Figuring Your Taxes, and Refundable and Nonrefundable Credits
Covers how to figure your tax and the credits you may be able to claim.

---

## What Is in This Publication

This publication covers:
- Filing requirements
- Filing status
- Dependents
- Tax withholding
- Types of income
- Adjustments to income
- Standard and itemized deductions
- Tax credits
- How to figure your tax

## What Is Not Covered in This Publication

This publication does not cover:
- Business income and expenses (see Pub. 334 for small businesses, Pub. 535 for business expenses)
- Farm income and expenses (see Pub. 225)
- Partnerships (see Pub. 541)
- Corporations (see Pub. 542)
- S corporations (see instructions for Form 1120-S)
- Estate and gift taxes (see Pub. 559 and Form 706/709 instructions)
