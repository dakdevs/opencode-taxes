# 2025 Alternative Minimum Tax (AMT) Worksheet

## Overview

The AMT is a parallel tax system that limits certain deductions and preferences. You pay the **higher** of regular tax or AMT.

---

## 2025 AMT Exemption Amounts

| Filing Status | Exemption |
|---------------|-----------|
| Single | $88,100 |
| Married Filing Jointly | $137,000 |
| Married Filing Separately | $68,500 |
| Head of Household | $88,100 |
| Trusts/Estates | $30,700 |

## AMT Exemption Phase-Out

The exemption phases out at 25% for income above these thresholds:

| Filing Status | Phase-Out Begins | Exemption Eliminated At |

## AMT Tax Rates

| AMTI (over exemption) | Rate |
|-----------------------|------|
| First $232,600 (MFJ) / $116,300 (other) | 26% |
| Above threshold | 28% |

---

## AMT Adjustments and Preferences

### Items That INCREASE AMTI

| Item | Treatment |
|------|-----------|
| State/local tax deduction | Add back entire amount |
| Miscellaneous itemized deductions | Add back |
| Standard deduction | Add back (if taken) |
| Personal exemptions | Add back |
| Medical expenses | Only deduct if > 7.5% AGI |
| Home equity loan interest | Add back (unless used for home improvement) |
| Incentive stock options (ISO) | Add bargain element on exercise |
| Depreciation (MACRS) | Recalculate using ADS |
| Private activity bond interest | Add back (with exceptions) |

### Items That REDUCE AMT
- AMT foreign tax credit
- Prior year minimum tax credit

## Who Is at Risk for AMT?

High-risk situations:
- Large state/local tax deductions (especially in high-tax states)
- Exercised incentive stock options (ISOs)
- Large long-term capital gains
- Many dependents/personal exemptions
- Large miscellaneous deductions
- Significant home equity loan interest (not for home improvement)
- Income from private activity bonds

---

## AMT Credit for Prior Years

If you paid AMT in prior years due to timing differences (like ISO exercise), you may have an AMT credit carryforward.

- Use **Form 8801** to calculate and claim
- Credit can offset regular tax (not below tentative minimum tax)
- Refundable portion may be available

---

## Planning Strategies

### To Reduce AMT Exposure:

1. **Time ISO exercises** - Exercise over multiple years
2. **Bunch deductions** - Alternate between standard and itemizing
3. **Consider tax-exempt bonds** - Regular muni bonds exempt from AMT
4. **Accelerate income** - If in low AMT year, recognize more income
5. **Defer deductions** - If in AMT year, preferences won't help anyway
