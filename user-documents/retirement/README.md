# Retirement Account Documentation

Place retirement-related documents in the appropriate subfolders.

## Why These Matter

Retirement contributions can:
- Reduce your taxable income (Traditional IRA, 401k)
- Qualify you for the Saver's Credit
- Provide tax-free growth (Roth accounts)

## Folder Contents

| Folder | Documents | Purpose |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| `5498/` | Form 5498 (IRA contributions) | Verify IRA deduction |
| `401k/` | Year-end statements | Verify 401k contributions |
| `hsa/` | Form 5498-SA | HSA contribution/deduction |

## 2025 Contribution Limits

| Account Type | Under 50 | 50 or Older | Ages 60-63 |
|--------------|----------|-------------|------------|
| 401(k)/403(b)/457 | $23,500 | $31,000 | $34,750 |
| Traditional/Roth IRA | $7,000 | $8,000 | $8,000 |
| SIMPLE IRA | $16,500 | $20,000 | $21,250 |
| SEP IRA | Up to $70,000 | Up to $70,000 | Up to $70,000 |
| HSA (self-only) | $4,300 | $5,300 | $5,300 |
| HSA (family) | $8,550 | $9,550 | $9,550 |

## Deductibility of IRA Contributions

Traditional IRA contributions may be deductible depending on:
- Whether you're covered by a workplace retirement plan
- Your filing status and income
- Whether your spouse is covered by a plan

See the IRA chapter in the reference guide for phase-out limits.
