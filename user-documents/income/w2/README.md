# W-2 Forms (Wage and Tax Statement)

Place your W-2 forms from all employers here.

## What Is a W-2?

Your employer sends you a W-2 if you were an employee (not a contractor) and earned wages during 2025.

## When to Expect It

Employers must send W-2s by **January 31, 2026**.

## What to Look For

| Box | Description | Used For |
|-----|-------------|----------|
| 1 | Wages, tips, other compensation | Your taxable wages |
| 2 | Federal income tax withheld | Tax already paid |
| 3 | Social Security wages | SS tax calculation |
| 4 | Social Security tax withheld | Tax already paid |
| 5 | Medicare wages and tips | Medicare tax calculation |
| 6 | Medicare tax withheld | Tax already paid |
| 12 | Various codes (retirement, etc.) | 401k contributions, etc. |
| 17 | State income tax | State tax withheld |

## Multiple W-2s?

If you had multiple jobs, place ALL W-2s in this folder. Each will be added to your return.

## Missing Your W-2?

1. Contact your employer's HR/payroll department
2. Check if available online through your employer's portal
3. Use your last pay stub as a reference (but still try to get the W-2)
4. Request a Wage and Income Transcript from IRS after February 15
