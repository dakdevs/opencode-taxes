## How to Use the Tax Tables

1. Find your taxable income in the left columns
2. Find the column for your filing status
3. The intersection shows your tax

> **Note:** Use the Tax Rate Schedules in [Chapter 13](../part4/chapter-13-figuring-tax.md) if your taxable income is $100,000 or more.

## Capital Gains Tax Rates

### Long-Term Capital Gains (Assets Held Over 1 Year)

| Tax Bracket | Rate |
|-------------|------|
| 10% or 12% | 0% |
| 22%, 24%, 32%, 35% | 15% |
| 37% | 20% |

### 2025 Long-Term Capital Gains Thresholds

| Filing Status | 0% Rate | 15% Rate | 20% Rate |
|--------------|---------|----------|----------|
| Single | $0 - $48,350 | $48,351 - $533,400 | Over $533,400 |
| MFJ/QSS | $0 - $96,700 | $96,701 - $600,050 | Over $600,050 |
| MFS | $0 - $48,350 | $48,351 - $300,025 | Over $300,025 |
| HOH | $0 - $64,750 | $64,751 - $566,700 | Over $566,700 |

### Short-Term Capital Gains

Taxed as ordinary income at your regular tax rate.

### Special Rates

| Type of Gain | Maximum Rate |
|--------------|--------------|
| Collectibles (art, coins, etc.) | 28% |
| Unrecaptured Section 1250 gain | 25% |
| Qualified Small Business Stock (Section 1202) | Various exclusions |

## Net Investment Income Tax

**Rate:** 3.8% on the lesser of:
- Net investment income, or
- Modified AGI exceeding threshold

| Filing Status | Threshold |
|--------------|-----------|
| Single | $200,000 |
| MFJ | $250,000 |
| MFS | $125,000 |
| HOH | $200,000 |
