# Schedule K-1 (Partner's/Shareholder's/Beneficiary's Share)

Place your K-1 forms here.

## What Is a Schedule K-1?

You receive a K-1 if you have ownership in:
- **Partnership** (Form 1065, Schedule K-1)
- **S Corporation** (Form 1120-S, Schedule K-1)
- **Estate or Trust** (Form 1041, Schedule K-1)

## Why K-1s Are Complex

K-1s pass through various types of income, deductions, and credits to you. Each item may be reported differently on your return.

## Common K-1 Items

### Partnership/S-Corp K-1

| Box | Description | Where Reported |

| 1 | Ordinary business income | Schedule E |
| 2 | Net rental real estate income | Schedule E |
| 3 | Other net rental income | Schedule E |

| 6a | Ordinary dividends | Schedule B |
| 7 | Royalties | Schedule E |
| 8 | Net short-term capital gain | Schedule D |
| 9a | Net long-term capital gain | Schedule D |
| 11 | Section 179 deduction | Form 4562 |
| 12 | Other deductions | Various |
| 13 | Credits | Various |

### Estate/Trust K-1

Similar structure but from Form 1041.

## Timing

K-1s are often issued late (March or later) because the entity must file its return first.

## Self-Employment Tax

Partnership K-1 income may be subject to self-employment tax (unlike S-corp distributions).
