# 1099-INT (Interest Income)

Place your 1099-INT forms here.

## What Is a 1099-INT?

Banks and financial institutions send you a 1099-INT if they paid you **$10 or more** in interest during 2025.

## Common Sources

- Savings accounts
- Checking accounts (if interest-bearing)
- Certificates of Deposit (CDs)
- Money market accounts
- U.S. Treasury securities
- Bonds

## What to Look For

| Box | Description | Tax Treatment |
|-----|-------------|---------------|
| 1 | Interest income | Taxable |
| 3 | Interest on U.S. Savings Bonds | May be excludable for education |
| 8 | Tax-exempt interest | Report but not taxed |
| 4 | Federal income tax withheld | Credit on your return |

## No 1099-INT?

You must still report interest income even if:
- You didn't receive a 1099-INT (under $10 threshold)
- The form was lost

Check your bank statements or online banking for annual interest summaries.
