## Introduction

In addition to the regular income tax, you may be subject to other taxes. These are reported on Schedule 2 (Form 1040).

---

## Self-Employment Tax

### What Is Self-Employment Tax?

Self-employment tax is Social Security and Medicare tax for people who work for themselves.

### Tax Rates (2025)

| Component | Rate | Wage Base |
|-----------|------|-----------|
| Social Security | 12.4% | $176,100 |
| Medicare | 2.9% | Unlimited |
| **Total** | **15.3%** | |

### Who Owes SE Tax?

You owe SE tax if you have net self-employment earnings of **$400 or more**.

Self-employment income includes:
- Sole proprietor income (Schedule C)
- Partnership income (your share)
- Farm income (Schedule F)
- Certain other income (director fees, etc.)

### Calculation

1. Net self-employment income × 92.35%
2. Apply rates to result
3. Deduct half of SE tax as adjustment to income

### Example

```
Schedule C net profit: $80,000
Step 1: $80,000 × 92.35% = $73,880
Step 2: Social Security: $73,880 × 12.4% = $9,161
        Medicare: $73,880 × 2.9% = $2,143
        Total SE tax: $11,304
Step 3: Deduction: $11,304 ÷ 2 = $5,652
```

### Schedule SE

Use **Schedule SE** to calculate self-employment tax.

## Additional Medicare Tax

### What Is It?

An additional 0.9% Medicare tax on wages and self-employment income above certain thresholds.

### Thresholds

| Filing Status | Threshold |
|--------------|-----------|
| Single | $200,000 |
| MFJ | $250,000 |
| MFS | $125,000 |
| HOH | $200,000 |

### Applies To

- Wages
- Self-employment income
- Railroad retirement compensation

### Withholding

Employers must withhold the additional 0.9% tax on wages over $200,000 (regardless of filing status).

You may need to:
- Pay additional tax if your income exceeds your threshold but is under $200,000
- Get a refund if too much was withheld

### Form 8959

Use **Form 8959** to calculate the Additional Medicare Tax.

## Net Investment Income Tax

### What Is the NIIT?

A 3.8% tax on net investment income for higher-income taxpayers.

### Thresholds (Same as Additional Medicare Tax)

| Filing Status | MAGI Threshold |
|--------------|----------------|
| Single | $200,000 |
| MFJ | $250,000 |
| MFS | $125,000 |
| HOH | $200,000 |

### What Is Net Investment Income?

**Includes:**
- Interest
- Dividends
- Capital gains
- Rental and royalty income
- Non-qualified annuities
- Passive activity income

**Excludes:**
- Wages and self-employment income
- Distributions from qualified retirement plans
- Tax-exempt interest
- Gain excluded from sale of main home

### Calculation

The tax is 3.8% of the **lesser of:**
- Net investment income, **or**
- MAGI exceeding the threshold

### Form 8960

Use **Form 8960** to calculate the Net Investment Income Tax.

## Household Employment Taxes

### When Do They Apply?

If you pay a household employee (nanny, housekeeper, caregiver) $2,700 or more in 2025.

### What's Included

| Tax | Rate | Wage Base |
|-----|------|-----------|
| Social Security | 6.2% each | $176,100 |
| Medicare | 1.45% each | Unlimited |
| FUTA (employer only) | 6% (0.6% after credit) | $7,000 |

### Schedule H

Use **Schedule H** to report household employment taxes.

### Not Household Employees

- Your spouse
- Your child under age 21
- Your parent (with exceptions)
- Anyone under age 18 whose principal occupation is student

## Early Distribution Penalties

### 10% Penalty on Early Distributions

Generally applies to distributions from:
- IRAs (traditional and Roth earnings)
- 401(k), 403(b), 457 plans
- Other qualified retirement plans

**Before age 59½**

### Exceptions to the 10% Penalty

| Exception | IRA | Employer Plan |
|-----------|-----|---------------|
| Death | Yes | Yes |
| Disability | Yes | Yes |
| Medical expenses > 7.5% AGI | Yes | Yes |
| Substantially equal periodic payments | Yes | Yes |
| Higher education expenses | Yes | No |
| First-time home purchase ($10K) | Yes | No |
| Health insurance premiums (unemployed) | Yes | No |
| IRS levy | Yes | Yes |
| Qualified reservist | Yes | Yes |
| Birth or adoption ($5K) | Yes | Yes |
| Domestic abuse victim ($10K) | Yes | Yes |
| Terminal illness | Yes | Yes |
| Separation from service at 55+ | No | Yes |
| QDRO distributions | No | Yes |

### Form 5329

Use **Form 5329** to report the additional tax or claim an exception.

## Alternative Minimum Tax

### What Is AMT?

A parallel tax system ensuring taxpayers with high income pay at least a minimum amount of tax.

### 2025 AMT Exemption

| Filing Status | Exemption |
|--------------|-----------|
| Single | $88,100 |
| MFJ/QSS | $137,000 |
| MFS | $68,500 |

### Phase-Out

| Filing Status | Phase-Out Begins |
|--------------|------------------|
| Single | $626,350 |
| MFJ/QSS | $1,252,700 |
| MFS | $626,350 |

### AMT Rates

| AMTI | Rate |
|------|------|
| First $232,600 ($116,300 MFS) | 26% |
| Over $232,600 | 28% |

### Common AMT Adjustments

- State and local tax deduction (add back)
- Miscellaneous deductions (add back)
- Incentive stock option exercise
- Private activity bond interest
- Certain depreciation

### Form 6251

Use **Form 6251** to calculate AMT.

## Other Taxes

### Excess Advance Premium Tax Credit Repayment

If you received more advance PTC than you qualify for, you must repay the excess.

**Form 8962**

### Individual Shared Responsibility Payment

**Note:** The payment is $0 for 2019 and later years, but some states have their own mandates.

### Recapture Taxes

May apply if you claimed certain credits and later don't meet requirements:
- First-time homebuyer credit recapture
- Investment credit recapture
- Low-income housing credit recapture

### Lump-Sum Social Security Payments

If you receive a lump-sum payment for prior years, you may be able to reduce your tax using a special calculation.

### Tax on Accumulation Distribution of Trusts

If you receive an accumulation distribution from a trust, additional tax may apply.

## Tax Calculation Order

When calculating your total tax:

1. **Regular tax** (from tax tables or schedules)
2. **Plus:** AMT (if applicable)
3. **Plus:** Other taxes (Schedule 2)
4. **Minus:** Credits
5. **Plus:** Self-employment tax, Additional Medicare Tax, NIIT
6. **Equals:** Total tax
