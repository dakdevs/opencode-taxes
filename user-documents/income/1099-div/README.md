# 1099-DIV (Dividends and Distributions)

Place your 1099-DIV forms here.

## What Is a 1099-DIV?

Brokerages, mutual funds, and corporations send you a 1099-DIV if they paid you **$10 or more** in dividends during 2025.

## What to Look For

| Box | Description | Tax Treatment |
|-----|-------------|---------------|
| 1a | Total ordinary dividends | Taxable as ordinary income |
| 1b | Qualified dividends | Lower tax rate (0%, 15%, or 20%) |
| 2a | Total capital gain distributions | Long-term capital gain rate |
| 3 | Nondividend distributions | Return of capital (reduces basis) |
| 4 | Federal income tax withheld | Credit on your return |
| 12 | Exempt-interest dividends | Tax-exempt (report but not taxed) |

## Qualified vs. Ordinary Dividends

**Qualified dividends** (Box 1b) are taxed at lower capital gains rates.
**Ordinary dividends** (Box 1a minus 1b) are taxed at your regular rate.

## Reinvested Dividends

Even if dividends were automatically reinvested, they are still taxable in the year received.
