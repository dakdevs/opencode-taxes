# 2025 Capital Gains Tax Rates

## Long-Term Capital Gains (Assets Held > 1 Year)

### Single Filers

| Taxable Income | Rate |
|----------------|------|
| $0 - $48,350 | 0% |
| $48,351 - $533,400 | 15% |
| Over $533,400 | 20% |

### Married Filing Jointly / Qualifying Surviving Spouse

| Taxable Income | Rate |
|----------------|------|
| $0 - $96,700 | 0% |
| $96,701 - $600,050 | 15% |
| Over $600,050 | 20% |

### Married Filing Separately

| Taxable Income | Rate |
|----------------|------|
| $0 - $48,350 | 0% |
| $48,351 - $300,025 | 15% |
| Over $300,025 | 20% |

### Head of Household

| Taxable Income | Rate |
|----------------|------|
| $0 - $64,750 | 0% |
| $64,751 - $566,700 | 15% |
| Over $566,700 | 20% |

---

## Short-Term Capital Gains (Assets Held ≤ 1 Year)

Taxed at **ordinary income rates** (see tax-brackets-2025.md).

---

## Special Capital Gains Rates

### Collectibles (28% Rate)
- Art, antiques, gems, stamps, coins
- Most metals (gold, silver, etc.)
- Taxed at maximum 28% rate

### Unrecaptured Section 1250 Gain (25% Rate)
- Depreciation recapture on real property
- Taxed at maximum 25% rate

### Qualified Small Business Stock (QSBS)
- Section 1202 exclusion
- May exclude 50%, 75%, or 100% of gain depending on acquisition date
- Must meet holding period and other requirements

---

## Net Investment Income Tax (NIIT)

An additional **3.8% tax** applies to capital gains if Modified AGI exceeds:

| Filing Status | Threshold |
|---------------|-----------|
| Single | $200,000 |
| Married Filing Jointly | $250,000 |
| Married Filing Separately | $125,000 |
| Head of Household | $200,000 |

**Effective Maximum Rate**: 20% + 3.8% = **23.8%**

---

## Capital Loss Rules

### Netting Process
1. Net short-term gains against short-term losses
2. Net long-term gains against long-term losses
3. Net results against each other

### Deduction Limits
- Net capital loss deduction: **$3,000/year** ($1,500 if MFS)
- Excess losses carry forward indefinitely

### Wash Sale Rule
Cannot deduct loss if you buy substantially identical securities within 30 days before or after the sale.

```
PART 1: Short-Term (held 1 year or less)

2. Total short-term losses:                       $_______

PART 2: Long-Term (held more than 1 year)
4. Total long-term gains:                         $_______
5. Total long-term losses:                        $_______
6. Net long-term (Line 4 - Line 5):               $_______

PART 3: Combined
7. Net capital gain/loss (Line 3 + Line 6):       $_______

If Line 7 is negative:
8. Capital loss deduction (max $3,000):           $_______
9. Carryover to next year (Line 7 + $3,000):      $_______

If Line 7 is positive:
- Short-term gains taxed at ordinary rates
- Long-term gains taxed at preferential rates above
```

## Cryptocurrency and Digital Assets

As of 2025, cryptocurrency is treated as property:
- **Short-term**: Held ≤ 1 year → ordinary income rates
- **Long-term**: Held > 1 year → capital gains rates above

Taxable events include:
- Selling crypto for fiat currency
- Trading one crypto for another
- Using crypto to purchase goods/services
- Receiving crypto as payment

**Form 1099-DA**: Starting in 2025, brokers may issue Form 1099-DA for digital asset transactions.
