# IRS Publication 17 (2025) - Your Federal Income Tax

**For Individuals - For use in preparing 2025 Returns**

> All material in this publication may be reprinted freely. A citation to Your Federal Income Tax (2025) would be appropriate.

The explanations and examples in this publication reflect the interpretation by the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) of:
- Tax laws enacted by Congress
- Treasury regulations

## Key 2025 Tax Changes

### Standard Deduction Amounts (2025)
| Filing Status | Amount |
|--------------|--------|
| Single or Married filing separately | $15,750 |
| Married filing jointly or Qualifying surviving spouse | $31,500 |
| Head of household | $23,625 |

### New Deductions for 2025
1. **No Tax on Tips** - Deduct up to $25,000 of qualified tips
2. **No Tax on Overtime** - Deduct up to $12,500 ($25,000 MFJ) of qualified overtime
3. **No Tax on Car Loan Interest** - Deduct up to $10,000 of qualified vehicle loan interest
4. **Enhanced Senior Deduction** - Up to $6,000 ($12,000 if both spouses eligible) for those born before January 2, 1961

### Other Key Changes
- **State and Local Tax Deduction Limit** increased to $40,000 ($20,000 MFS)
- **Trump Account** - New IRA type for children born 2025-2028 with $1,000 pilot contribution option
- **401(k)/403(b) Contribution Limits**: $23,500 (or $31,000 if 50+, or $37,750 if ages 60-63)
- **Higher Catch-up Contributions** for ages 60-63: $11,250
