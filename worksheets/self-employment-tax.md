# 2025 Self-Employment Tax Worksheet

## Overview

Self-employment (SE) tax consists of:
- **Social Security**: 12.4% on first $176,100 of net earnings
- **Medicare**: 2.9% on all net earnings
- **Additional Medicare**: 0.9% on earnings over threshold

**Total SE Tax Rate**: 15.3% (up to Social Security wage base)

---

## 2025 Thresholds

| Component | Rate | Wage Base/Threshold |
|-----------|------|---------------------|
| Social Security | 12.4% | $176,100 |
| Medicare | 2.9% | No limit |
| Additional Medicare | 0.9% | Over $200,000 (single) |

## Important Notes

### When SE Tax Applies
- Net earnings from self-employment of $400 or more
- Church employee income of $108.28 or more

### Reducing SE Tax
1. **Business deductions**: Reduce net profit before calculating SE tax
2. **Retirement contributions**: SEP-IRA, Solo 401(k) reduce income tax but not SE tax
3. **Health insurance deduction**: Self-employed health insurance deduction (income tax only)

### Forms Required
- **Schedule SE**: Calculate self-employment tax
- **Schedule C**: Report business profit/loss
- **Form 1040**: Line 23 (SE tax)
- **Schedule 1**: Line 15 (deductible portion)

### Quarterly Estimated Payments
Self-employed individuals typically must make quarterly estimated tax payments:
- April 15, 2025
- June 16, 2025
- September 15, 2025
- January 15, 2026

Use Form 1040-ES to calculate and pay quarterly estimates.
