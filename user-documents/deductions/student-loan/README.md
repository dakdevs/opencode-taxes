# Student Loan Interest (Form 1098-E)

Place your Form 1098-E here.

## What Is Form 1098-E?

Student loan servicers send Form 1098-E if you paid **$600 or more** in student loan interest.

## Special: Adjustment to Income

Student loan interest is an **adjustment to income** (Schedule 1), NOT an itemized deduction.

This means you can claim it even if you take the standard deduction!

## Deduction Limits

- **Maximum deduction:** $2,500 per year
- Cannot exceed the interest actually paid

## Income Phase-Out (2025)

| Filing Status | Phase-Out Begins | Complete Phase-Out |
|--------------|------------------|-------------------|
| Single/HOH | $80,000 | $95,000 |
| MFJ | $165,000 | $195,000 |
| MFS | Not allowed | Not allowed |

## What Qualifies

Interest on loans used for:
- Tuition and fees
- Room and board
- Books and supplies
- Other necessary education expenses

The loan must have been for you, your spouse, or your dependent.

## What Doesn't Qualify

- Interest on loans from family members
- Interest on loans from qualified employer plans
- Interest on loans that aren't for qualified education expenses

## What to Look For on Form 1098-E

| Box | Description |
|-----|-------------|
| 1 | Student loan interest received |
| 2 | Checkbox if Box 1 doesn't include loan origination fees |
