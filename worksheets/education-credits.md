# 2025 Education Tax Credits Worksheet

## Overview

Two main education credits available:
1. **American Opportunity Tax Credit (AOTC)** - For first 4 years of college
2. **Lifetime Learning Credit (LLC)** - For any higher education

**Cannot claim both for the same student in the same year.**

---

## American Opportunity Tax Credit (AOTC)

### Credit Amount
- **100%** of first $2,000 of qualified expenses
- **25%** of next $2,000
- **Maximum**: $2,500 per eligible student

### Refundable Portion
- **40%** of credit is refundable (up to $1,000)

### Eligibility Requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| Enrollment | At least half-time in degree program |
| Year in school | First 4 years of postsecondary education |
| Prior claims | Not claimed AOTC for 4+ tax years |
| Felony drug conviction | None at end of tax year |
| Student SSN | Valid SSN required (not ITIN) |

### Income Phase-Out (2025)

| Filing Status | Full Credit | Phase-Out Range | No Credit |
|---------------|-------------|-----------------|-----------|
| Single/HOH | ≤ $80,000 | $80,001 - $90,000 | > $90,000 |
| Married Filing Jointly | ≤ $160,000 | $160,001 - $180,000 | > $180,000 |
| Married Filing Separately | N/A | N/A | Not eligible |

---

## Lifetime Learning Credit (LLC)

### Credit Amount
- **20%** of first $10,000 of qualified expenses
- **Maximum**: $2,000 per tax return (not per student)

### Eligibility Requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| Enrollment | At least one course |
| Year in school | Any year, any number of years |
| Degree required | No (skills courses eligible) |
| Felony drug conviction | No restriction |

### Income Phase-Out (2025)

| Filing Status | Full Credit | Phase-Out Range | No Credit |
|---------------|-------------|-----------------|-----------|
| Single/HOH | ≤ $80,000 | $80,001 - $90,000 | > $90,000 |
| Married Filing Jointly | ≤ $160,000 | $160,001 - $180,000 | > $180,000 |
| Married Filing Separately | N/A | N/A | Not eligible |

---

## Qualified Education Expenses

### Included
- Tuition and fees required for enrollment
- Books, supplies, and equipment (AOTC only - even if not purchased from school)
- Course materials required for enrollment (LLC)

### Excluded
- Room and board
- Insurance
- Medical expenses
- Transportation
- Personal expenses
- Sports, games, hobbies (unless part of degree)

### Must Reduce Expenses By:
- Tax-free scholarships and grants
- Employer-provided educational assistance
- Veterans' educational benefits
- Any other tax-free payments

## Comparison: AOTC vs LLC

| Feature | AOTC | LLC |
|---------|------|-----|
| Maximum credit | $2,500/student | $2,000/return |
| Credit rate | 100%/25% | 20% |
| Expense limit | $4,000 | $10,000 |
| Refundable | 40% (up to $1,000) | No |
| Year limit | 4 years | Unlimited |
| Enrollment | Half-time minimum | One course |
| Degree required | Yes | No |
| Felony drug | Disqualifies | No effect |

### When to Choose Each

**Choose AOTC when**:
- First 4 years of undergraduate
- Enrolled at least half-time
- Credit amount would be higher
- You have little/no tax liability (refundable portion helps)

**Choose LLC when**:
- Graduate school
- Already claimed AOTC 4 times
- Part-time or continuing education
- Non-degree courses

---

## Multiple Students

- **AOTC**: Calculate separately for each eligible student, then add
- **LLC**: Combine all expenses, apply 20% (max $2,000 total)

### Example: 2 Students

```
AOTC approach:
  Student 1: $2,500 credit
  Student 2: $2,500 credit
  Total: $5,000

LLC approach:
  Combined expenses: $20,000
  Maximum eligible: $10,000
  Credit: $2,000

→ AOTC is better (if both students qualify)
```

---

## Coordination with Other Benefits

Cannot use same expenses for:
- Education credits AND tuition/fees deduction (repealed)
- Tax-free distribution from 529 plan
- Tax-free distribution from Coverdell ESA
- Tax-free employer assistance

**Strategy**: Allocate 529/ESA to room and board, use other expenses for credit.

## Forms Required

- **Form 8863**: Education Credits
- **Form 1098-T**: Tuition Statement (from school)
- **Form 1040**: Line 29 (nonrefundable) and Line 29 (refundable AOTC)

## Record Keeping

Keep for at least 3 years:
- Form 1098-T
- Receipts for books and supplies
- Proof of payment
- Records of scholarships/grants received
- Documentation of enrollment status
