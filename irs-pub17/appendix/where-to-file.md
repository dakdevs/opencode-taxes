## E-File Is Recommended

The IRS recommends e-filing your return:
- Faster processing
- Fewer errors
- Immediate confirmation
- Faster refunds (especially with direct deposit)

**E-file options:**
- IRS Free File (IRS.gov/FreeFile)
- Tax preparation software
- Tax professional

## Filing Tips

### Before Mailing

- [ ] Sign and date your return
- [ ] Attach all required schedules and forms
- [ ] Attach Form W-2 copies
- [ ] Include payment voucher (Form 1040-V) if paying by check
- [ ] Make check payable to "United States Treasury"
- [ ] Write your SSN, phone number, tax year, and "Form 1040" on check
- [ ] Use correct postage

### Mailing Your Return

- Use certified mail with return receipt for proof of filing
- Keep copies of everything you mail
- Mail early to avoid last-minute issues

### Timely Filing

Your return is considered timely filed if:
- Postmarked by the due date (April 15, 2026)
- Delivered by a private delivery service by the due date
