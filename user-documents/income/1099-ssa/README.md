# SSA-1099 (Social Security Benefits)

Place your SSA-1099 forms here.

## What Is SSA-1099?

The Social Security Administration sends SSA-1099 if you received Social Security benefits during 2025.

## What to Look For

| Box | Description | Notes |
|-----|-------------|-------|
| 3 | Benefits paid in 2025 | Gross benefits |
| 4 | Benefits repaid | If you repaid any benefits |
| 5 | Net benefits | Box 3 minus Box 4 |
| 6 | Voluntary tax withholding | Credit on return |

## Are Social Security Benefits Taxable?

**Maybe.** Up to 85% of your benefits may be taxable depending on your combined income.

### Combined Income Formula
```
Combined Income = AGI + Nontaxable Interest + ½ of Social Security Benefits
```

### Taxability Thresholds

| Filing Status | Combined Income | Amount Taxable |
|--------------|-----------------|----------------|
| Single/HOH | Under $25,000 | 0% |
| Single/HOH | $25,000 - $34,000 | Up to 50% |
| Single/HOH | Over $34,000 | Up to 85% |
| MFJ | Under $32,000 | 0% |
| MFJ | $32,000 - $44,000 | Up to 50% |
| MFJ | Over $44,000 | Up to 85% |

## RRB-1099

If you receive Railroad Retirement benefits, you'll get RRB-1099 instead. Place those here as well.
