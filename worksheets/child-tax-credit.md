# 2025 Child Tax Credit (CTC) Worksheet

## Credit Amounts

| Credit Type | Amount | Age Requirement |
|-------------|--------|-----------------|
| Child Tax Credit | $2,000 | Under 17 at end of year |
| Credit for Other Dependents | $500 | Any age (if not qualifying for CTC) |

### Refundable Portion (Additional Child Tax Credit)
- Maximum refundable: $1,700 per qualifying child
- Must have earned income over $2,500

---

## Qualifying Child Requirements

A child must meet ALL of these tests:

### 1. Age Test
- Under age 17 at end of tax year (December 31, 2025)

### 2. Relationship Test
- Son, daughter, stepchild, foster child, adopted child
- Brother, sister, stepbrother, stepsister
- Descendant of any of the above (grandchild, niece, nephew)

### 3. Residency Test
- Lived with you for more than half the year
- Temporary absences count as living with you

### 4. Support Test
- Child did not provide more than half of their own support

### 5. Dependent Test
- You must claim the child as a dependent on your return

### 6. Citizenship Test
- U.S. citizen, U.S. national, or U.S. resident alien
- Must have valid Social Security Number by return due date

## Income Phase-Out

The credit phases out at higher income levels.

### Phase-Out Thresholds

| Filing Status | Phase-Out Begins | Phase-Out Rate |
|---------------|------------------|----------------|
| Single | $200,000 | $50 per $1,000 |
| Head of Household | $200,000 | $50 per $1,000 |
| Married Filing Jointly | $400,000 | $50 per $1,000 |
| Married Filing Separately | $200,000 | $50 per $1,000 |

## Forms Required

- **Schedule 8812**: Credits for Qualifying Children and Other Dependents
- **Form 1040**: Lines 19 (nonrefundable) and 28 (refundable)

## Common Issues

1. **SSN Required**: Child must have SSN (not ITIN) for the $2,000 credit
2. **ITIN Children**: Only eligible for $500 credit for other dependents
3. **Split Custody**: Only one parent can claim the credit per child
4. **Age Cutoff**: Child must be under 17 on December 31, 2025
