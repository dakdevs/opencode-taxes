## Introduction

The child tax credit helps families with qualifying children. There are three related credits:

| Credit | Maximum Amount | Refundable? |
|--------|---------------|-------------|
| Child Tax Credit | $2,000 per child | No (nonrefundable) |
| Additional Child Tax Credit | $1,700 per child | Yes |
| Credit for Other Dependents | $500 per dependent | No (nonrefundable) |

---

## Child Tax Credit

### Maximum Credit

**$2,000** per qualifying child

### Qualifying Child Requirements

To qualify for the child tax credit, the child must:

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| **Age** | Under 17 at end of tax year |
| **Relationship** | Your son, daughter, stepchild, foster child, sibling, or descendant |
| **Residency** | Lived with you for more than half the year |
| **Support** | Did not provide more than half of their own support |
| **Citizenship** | U.S. citizen, U.S. national, or U.S. resident alien |
| **SSN** | Must have a valid Social Security Number issued before return due date |
| **Dependent** | Must be claimed as your dependent |

### SSN Requirement

**Critical:** The child must have a valid SSN issued **before the due date of your return (including extensions)**.

- An ITIN does NOT qualify for the child tax credit
- An ATIN (Adoption Taxpayer Identification Number) does NOT qualify

> **Note:** If your child has an ITIN, they may qualify for the Credit for Other Dependents instead.

### Your SSN/ITIN Requirement (2025)

To claim the child tax credit, you (or your spouse if filing jointly) must have a valid SSN or ITIN issued before the return due date.

### Income Phase-Out

The credit phases out for higher incomes:

| Filing Status | Phase-Out Begins | Reduction |
|--------------|------------------|-----------|
| MFJ | $400,000 | $50 per $1,000 over threshold |
| All others | $200,000 | $50 per $1,000 over threshold |

### Phase-Out Calculation

```
Modified AGI: $450,000 (MFJ)
Threshold: $400,000
Excess: $50,000

Reduction: $50,000 / $1,000 = 50 × $50 = $2,500

If you have 2 qualifying children:
Total credit before phase-out: 2 × $2,000 = $4,000
Minus reduction: $4,000 - $2,500 = $1,500
Credit available: $1,500
```

### Nonrefundable Nature

The child tax credit is **nonrefundable**, meaning it can reduce your tax to zero but won't result in a refund by itself.

However, if you have remaining child tax credit after reducing your tax to zero, you may qualify for the Additional Child Tax Credit.

## Additional Child Tax Credit (ACTC)

### What Is the ACTC?

The ACTC is the **refundable portion** of the child tax credit. This means it can result in a refund even if you don't owe any tax.

### Maximum ACTC

**$1,700** per qualifying child (for 2025)

### How It Works

The ACTC is calculated based on your **earned income**:

1. Calculate 15% of earned income exceeding $2,500
2. Compare to unused child tax credit
3. ACTC is the lesser of: (a) unused child tax credit, or (b) 15% calculation, up to $1,700 per child

### Earned Income Requirement

Earned income includes:
- Wages, salaries, tips
- Net self-employment income
- Other taxable employee compensation

Does NOT include:
- Interest and dividends
- Social Security benefits
- Unemployment compensation
- Pensions

### Who Can't Claim ACTC

You cannot claim the ACTC if you:
- File Form 2555 (Foreign Earned Income)
- File Form 2555-EZ
- Are a bona fide resident of Puerto Rico claiming the ACTC exclusively based on 3+ qualifying children (different rules apply)

### Puerto Rico Residents

Bona fide residents of Puerto Rico may be eligible for the ACTC with **one or more** qualifying children (no longer requires 3+).

### Refund Timing

**Important:** Refunds for returns claiming the ACTC cannot be issued before **mid-February** of the year following the tax year.

## Credit for Other Dependents

### Maximum Credit

**$500** per qualifying dependent

### Who Qualifies

The credit for other dependents is for dependents who:
- Don't qualify for the child tax credit, **AND**
- Are claimed as your dependent

This includes:
- Children age 17 or older
- Children without a valid SSN (who have an ITIN)
- Qualifying relatives
- Parents or other dependent relatives

### Your SSN/ITIN Requirement (2025 Change)

**New for 2025:** To claim this credit, you (or your spouse if filing jointly) must have a valid SSN or ITIN issued before the return due date.

### Same Phase-Out Rules

The credit for other dependents uses the same phase-out thresholds as the child tax credit:

| Filing Status | Phase-Out Begins |
|--------------|------------------|
| MFJ | $400,000 |
| All others | $200,000 |

### Nonrefundable

The credit for other dependents is **nonrefundable**. There is no additional credit for other dependents like the ACTC.

---

## Comparison: Child Tax Credit vs. Credit for Other Dependents

| Feature | Child Tax Credit | Credit for Other Dependents |
|---------|-----------------|----------------------------|
| Maximum amount | $2,000 | $500 |
| Child's age | Under 17 | Any age |
| Child's SSN required | Yes | No (ITIN okay) |
| Refundable | Yes (via ACTC) | No |
| Phase-out starts | $400K MFJ / $200K others | Same |

## How to Claim

### Required Information

You'll need:
- Dependent's name
- Dependent's SSN or ITIN
- Dependent's relationship to you
- Months lived in your home

### Form 1040 Changes (2025)

The Dependents section now has:
- Numbered rows
- Additional questions about dependents
- Check boxes for credit eligibility

### Schedule 8812

Use **Schedule 8812** (Credits for Qualifying Children and Other Dependents) to:
- Calculate the child tax credit
- Calculate the credit for other dependents
- Calculate the additional child tax credit

### Step-by-Step

1. **Enter dependents** on Form 1040 (or 1040-SR)
2. **Check boxes** indicating which credits each qualifies for
3. **Complete Schedule 8812** to calculate your credits
4. **Enter credits** on Form 1040:
   - Line 19: Child tax credit and credit for other dependents
   - Line 28: Additional child tax credit (refundable)

## Common Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Child Under 17 with SSN
- Child tax credit: Up to $2,000
- ACTC: Up to $1,700 (if unused credit remains)

### Scenario 2: Child Under 17 with ITIN
- Child tax credit: $0 (SSN required)
- Credit for other dependents: Up to $500

### Scenario 3: Child Age 17 with SSN
- Child tax credit: $0 (must be under 17)
- Credit for other dependents: Up to $500

### Scenario 4: Elderly Parent as Dependent
- Child tax credit: $0 (not a child)
- Credit for other dependents: Up to $500

### Scenario 5: High-Income Taxpayer (MFJ, $500K MAGI)
- Phase-out reduction: ($500K - $400K) / $1,000 × $50 = $5,000
- If 2 children: $4,000 - $5,000 = $0 credit remaining
- Credits phased out completely

## Special Rules

### Multiple Support Agreements

Only the person who claims the dependent can claim the credit.

### Divorced or Separated Parents

- Custodial parent generally claims the credit
- If Form 8332 is signed, noncustodial parent can claim
- The noncustodial parent CAN claim child tax credit if custodial parent signs Form 8332
- But custodial parent retains right to claim EITC

### Children of Divorced Parents

| Benefit | Who Can Claim |
|---------|---------------|
| Child Tax Credit | Parent who claims child as dependent |
| ACTC | Parent who claims child as dependent |
| EITC | Custodial parent only |
| Child & Dependent Care Credit | Custodial parent only |
| Head of Household | Custodial parent only |
