# Property Tax Statements

Place your property tax bills and payment records here.

## What Qualifies

Real estate taxes on property you own:
- Main home
- Second home
- Vacant land

## What Doesn't Qualify

- Taxes for local improvements (sidewalks, streets)
- HOA fees
- Transfer taxes
- Taxes on property you don't own

## SALT Limitation

Property taxes are part of the **State and Local Tax (SALT)** deduction, which is limited to:
- **$40,000** (most filers)
- **$20,000** (married filing separately)

This limit applies to the TOTAL of:
- State income tax OR sales tax
- Local income tax
- Real estate taxes
- Personal property taxes

## What to Include

- Annual property tax statement from your county
- Escrow statements showing taxes paid (if taxes are escrowed)
- Receipts for direct tax payments

## Proration When Buying/Selling

If you bought or sold property during the year, taxes are prorated between buyer and seller based on ownership days.
