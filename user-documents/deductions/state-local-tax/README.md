# State and Local Tax Records

Place your state and local tax payment documentation here.

## What's Included in SALT

- State income tax withheld (from W-2)
- State estimated tax payments
- Local income taxes
- Prior year state tax paid in current year
- **OR** State and local sales tax (instead of income tax)
- Paid family leave contributions (new for 2025)

## 2025 SALT Limit

| Filing Status | Maximum Deduction |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Single, MFJ, HOH, QSS | $40,000 |
| Married Filing Separately | $20,000 |

**Phase-out:** Limit may be reduced if MAGI exceeds $500,000 ($250,000 MFS)

## Income Tax vs. Sales Tax

You can deduct either:
- State and local **income** taxes, OR
- State and local **sales** taxes

Choose whichever is greater. Most people choose income tax.

## Sales Tax Option

If you live in a state with no income tax (TX, FL, WA, etc.), choose sales tax.

Options for calculating sales tax:
1. IRS sales tax tables (based on income)
2. Actual receipts (requires keeping all receipts)

Add sales tax on major purchases (vehicles, boats, building materials) to the table amount.

## What to Include

- W-2 showing state tax withheld (Box 17)
- Estimated tax payment records
- Prior year state return showing balance due paid
- State tax payment confirmations
- Paid family leave contribution records
