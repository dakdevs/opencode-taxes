# Form 5498 (IRA Contribution Information)

Place your Form 5498 here.

## What Is Form 5498?

Your IRA custodian (Fidelity, Vanguard, Schwab, etc.) sends Form 5498 showing your IRA contributions for the year.

## Timing

Form 5498 is sent by **May 31** (after the contribution deadline), so you may not have it when you file.

You can file based on your own records and verify with Form 5498 when received.

## What to Look For

| Box | Description | Notes |
|-----|-------------|-------|
| 1 | IRA contributions | Traditional IRA contributions |
| 2 | Rollover contributions | Rollovers into this IRA |
| 3 | Roth IRA conversion | Converted from Traditional |
| 5 | FMV of account | Year-end value |
| 7 | IRA type | Traditional, Roth, SEP, SIMPLE |
| 8 | SEP contributions | Employer SEP contributions |
| 9 | SIMPLE contributions | SIMPLE IRA contributions |
| 10 | Roth IRA contributions | Your Roth contributions |
| 11 | Required minimum distribution | RMD for next year |
| 12a | RMD date | When RMDs begin |

## Contribution Deadline

You have until **April 15, 2026** (tax filing deadline) to make IRA contributions for 2025.

## Traditional vs. Roth

| Traditional IRA | Roth IRA |
|-----------------|----------|
| May be deductible | Never deductible |
| Taxed on withdrawal | Tax-free withdrawal (if qualified) |
| RMDs required | No RMDs during lifetime |
| Box 1 | Box 10 |
