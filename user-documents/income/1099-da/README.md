# 1099-DA (Digital Asset Transactions) - NEW FOR 2025

Place your 1099-DA forms here.

## What Is a 1099-DA?

Starting in 2025, cryptocurrency exchanges and digital asset brokers send 1099-DA when you **sell or dispose** of digital assets.

## Digital Assets Include

- Cryptocurrency (Bitcoin, Ethereum, etc.)
- Stablecoins
- NFTs (Non-Fungible Tokens)
- Other virtual currencies

## What to Look For

| Information | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| Asset sold | Type of digital asset |
| Date acquired | When you obtained it |
| Date sold | When you disposed of it |
| Proceeds | Amount received |
| Cost basis | What you paid (may be blank in 2025) |

## Important Notes for 2025

**Basis reporting is optional in 2025.** Brokers may report your cost basis but are not required to. You may need to calculate it yourself.

## Digital Asset Question on Form 1040

You MUST answer the digital asset question on page 1 of Form 1040, even if you received a 1099-DA.

**Answer "Yes" if you:**
- Sold digital assets
- Exchanged one crypto for another
- Received crypto as payment
- Received airdrops or staking rewards

## Record Keeping

Keep detailed records:
- Date of each purchase
- Purchase price
- Date of each sale
- Sale price
- Transaction fees (add to basis)
- Exchange/wallet used
