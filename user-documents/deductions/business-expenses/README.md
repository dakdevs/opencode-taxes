# Business Expenses (Schedule C)

Place your self-employment business expense documentation here.

## Who Uses This

If you have income from:
- Freelancing (1099-NEC)
- Sole proprietorship
- Gig economy (Uber, DoorDash, etc.)
- Side business

## Deductible Business Expenses

### Common Expenses

| Expense | Examples |
|---------|----------|
| Advertising | Website, business cards, ads |
| Car and truck | Mileage or actual expenses |
| Commissions | Fees paid to others |
| Contract labor | Subcontractors |
| Insurance | Business liability, E&O |
| Interest | Business loans, credit cards |
| Legal/Professional | Attorney, accountant fees |
| Office expense | Supplies, postage |
| Rent | Office space |
| Repairs | Equipment maintenance |
| Supplies | Materials for business |
| Travel | Business trips |
| Meals | 50% deductible (business purpose) |
| Utilities | If home office |

### Home Office Deduction

If you use part of your home exclusively and regularly for business:
- **Simplified method:** $5 per square foot (max 300 sq ft = $1,500)
- **Regular method:** Actual expenses × business use percentage

### Vehicle Expenses

Choose one method:
- **Standard mileage:** 70¢ per mile (2025)
- **Actual expenses:** Gas, insurance, repairs, depreciation × business use %

Keep a mileage log!

## What to Include

- Receipts for all expenses
- Mileage log
- Home office measurements
- Bank/credit card statements showing business purchases
- Contracts with clients
- Invoices issued
