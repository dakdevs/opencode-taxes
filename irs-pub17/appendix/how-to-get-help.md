## Preparing and Filing Your Return

### Tax Reform Information

For the latest tax law changes:
- Visit [IRS.gov/TaxReform](https://www.irs.gov/taxreform)
- Check [IRS.gov/Pub17](https://www.irs.gov/pub17) for updates

### E-File Options

E-filing is the fastest, most accurate way to file:
- Fewer errors than paper returns
- Faster refunds (especially with direct deposit)
- Confirmation that IRS received your return

## Free Tax Preparation Options

### IRS Free File

**If your AGI is $84,000 or less:**
- Use free brand-name software
- Guided preparation
- E-file included
- Visit [IRS.gov/FreeFile](https://www.irs.gov/freefile)

**If your AGI is over $84,000:**
- Use Free File Fillable Forms
- Electronic versions of IRS forms
- Basic calculations only

### Volunteer Income Tax Assistance (VITA)

Free tax help for:
- People who earn $67,000 or less
- Persons with disabilities
- Limited English speakers

**Find a VITA site:** [IRS.gov/VITA](https://www.irs.gov/vita)

### Tax Counseling for the Elderly (TCE)

Free tax help for taxpayers age 60 and older:
- Specializes in retirement issues
- AARP Tax-Aide sites included

**Find a TCE site:** 1-888-227-7669

### MilTax

Free tax services for military members:
- Tax preparation software
- Consultations with tax professionals
- Available to most military service members

**Visit:** [MilitaryOneSource.mil/MilTax](https://www.militaryonesource.mil/miltax)

## Online Tools

### Tax Withholding Estimator

Estimate your federal income tax withholding:
- Update your Form W-4
- Avoid surprises at tax time
- [IRS.gov/W4App](https://www.irs.gov/w4app)

### Interactive Tax Assistant (ITA)

Get answers to tax law questions:
- Filing requirements
- Credits you may qualify for
- Deduction eligibility
- [IRS.gov/ITA](https://www.irs.gov/ita)

### Where's My Refund?

Check your refund status:
- Online: [IRS.gov/Refunds](https://www.irs.gov/refunds)
- Mobile app: IRS2Go
- Phone: 1-800-829-1954

### Online Account

Access your tax information:
- View balance due
- Payment history
- Tax records
- [IRS.gov/Account](https://www.irs.gov/account)

### Get Transcript

Request your tax return transcript:
- Online: Immediate access
- By mail: 5-10 days
- [IRS.gov/Transcript](https://www.irs.gov/transcript)

### Direct Pay

Pay your taxes directly from your bank account:
- Free
- Secure
- [IRS.gov/DirectPay](https://www.irs.gov/directpay)

### EFTPS

Electronic Federal Tax Payment System:
- Schedule payments in advance
- View payment history
- Requires enrollment
- [EFTPS.gov](https://www.eftps.gov)

## Getting Forms and Publications

### Online

Download forms, instructions, and publications:
- [IRS.gov/Forms](https://www.irs.gov/forms)
- Available 24/7
- Current and prior years

### By Phone

Order forms and publications:
- Call 1-800-829-3676
- Allow 7-10 business days for delivery

### Mobile-Friendly Forms

IRS has mobile-friendly versions of many forms that work on smartphones and tablets.

### eBooks

Get publications in eBook format:
- For e-readers and mobile devices
- Available on IRS.gov

### In Person

**Taxpayer Assistance Centers (TACs):**
- Services by appointment only
- Schedule at [IRS.gov/TACLocator](https://www.irs.gov/taclocator)
- Some services available as walk-ins

**New for 2025:** You may receive appointment confirmations and reminders via text message.

### By Mail

**For tax payments:** Use the address on your payment voucher

**For correspondence:** Use the address on the notice you received

**For returns:** See [Where To File](./where-to-file.md)

## Tax Professionals

### Need Someone to Prepare Your Return?

Consider using a tax professional if:
- Your situation is complex
- You have limited time
- You want professional advice

### Finding a Tax Professional

**Enrolled Agents:** Licensed by IRS
- Search: [IRS.gov/Enrolled-Agent](https://www.irs.gov/enrolled-agent)

**CPAs:** Licensed by state
- Search: [AICPA.org](https://www.aicpa.org)

**Tax Attorneys:** Licensed to practice law
- Useful for legal tax matters

### Verify Credentials

Check preparer credentials:
- [IRS.gov/DirectorySearch](https://www.irs.gov/directorysearch)
- Look up PTIN status

### Tax Pro Account

Tax professionals can access your information with authorization:
- Submit Form 2848 (Power of Attorney)
- Submit Form 8821 (Tax Information Authorization)

## Taxpayer Advocate Service

### What Is TAS?

An independent organization within the IRS that helps taxpayers:
- Resolve problems with the IRS
- Address systemic issues
- Protect taxpayer rights

### When to Contact TAS

- Your problem is causing financial difficulty
- You've tried to resolve through normal channels
- You haven't received a response or resolution
- An IRS procedure isn't working as intended

### How to Contact TAS

**Online:** [TaxpayerAdvocate.IRS.gov](https://www.taxpayeradvocate.irs.gov)

**Phone:** 1-877-777-4778

**Form:** 911, Request for Taxpayer Advocate Service Assistance

**In Person:** TAS offices in every state

### Low Income Taxpayer Clinics (LITCs)

LITCs provide:
- Free or low-cost representation
- Help with audits, appeals, and collections
- Education about taxpayer rights

**Find an LITC:** [TaxpayerAdvocate.IRS.gov/LITC](https://www.taxpayeradvocate.irs.gov/litc)
