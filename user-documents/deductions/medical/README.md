# Medical and Dental Expenses

Place medical expense receipts and statements here.

## Important Threshold

Medical expenses are deductible only to the extent they exceed **7.5% of your AGI**.

### Example
```
AGI: $80,000
7.5% threshold: $6,000
Medical expenses: $8,000
Deductible amount: $2,000 ($8,000 - $6,000)
```

## What Qualifies

### Medical Care
- Doctor visits and specialist fees
- Hospital bills
- Surgery
- Prescription medications
- Insulin
- Medical equipment (wheelchairs, hearing aids, etc.)
- Lab fees and tests
- Mental health treatment
- Substance abuse treatment
- Long-term care services

### Dental Care
- Dental exams and cleanings
- Fillings, crowns, bridges
- Dentures
- Orthodontics

### Vision Care
- Eye exams
- Glasses
- Contact lenses
- LASIK surgery

### Insurance Premiums
- Health insurance premiums (if not pre-tax through employer)
- Medicare Part B and D premiums
- Long-term care insurance (limited by age)

### Transportation
- Mileage to medical appointments (21¢/mile for 2025)
- Parking and tolls for medical visits
- Ambulance services

## What Doesn't Qualify

- Cosmetic procedures (unless medically necessary)
- Gym memberships (usually)
- Toiletries and general health items
- Insurance premiums paid with pre-tax dollars
- Over-the-counter medications (except insulin)

## What to Include

- EOBs (Explanation of Benefits) from insurance
- Bills from doctors, hospitals, dentists
- Pharmacy receipts for prescriptions
- Health insurance premium statements
- Mileage log for medical travel
