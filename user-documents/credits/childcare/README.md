# Child and Dependent Care Credit

Place childcare provider information and receipts here.

## What Is This Credit?

A credit for expenses you pay for care of a qualifying person so you (and spouse) can work or look for work.

## Maximum Credit

| Number of Qualifying Persons | Max Expenses | Max Credit (20-35%) |
|------------------------------|--------------|---------------------|
| 1 | $3,000 | $600 - $1,050 |
| 2 or more | $6,000 | $1,200 - $2,100 |

Credit percentage depends on your AGI (35% for lowest incomes, 20% for AGI over $43,000).

## Who Is a Qualifying Person?

- Your dependent child under age 13
- Your spouse who can't care for themselves
- A dependent of any age who can't care for themselves

## Qualifying Expenses

- Daycare center
- Babysitter
- Nanny
- Au pair
- Before/after school care
- Day camp

## NOT Qualifying Expenses

- Overnight camp
- School tuition (kindergarten and above)
- Food and clothing
- Medical care
- Payments to your child under 19
- Payments to someone you claim as a dependent

## What to Include

For each care provider:
- **Name**
- **Address**
- **Taxpayer ID** (SSN or EIN)
- **Amount paid**

You'll need this information for Form 2441.

## Dependent Care FSA

If you have a Dependent Care FSA through work, those pre-tax amounts reduce the expenses eligible for this credit.
